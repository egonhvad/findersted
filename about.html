Finder Sted

Archive About

Finder Sted is an archive of student work, workshops, and events at the Institute of Building Arts and Culture, Kgl. Akademi — Arkitektur.

The archive documents the teaching and research activities of the Finder Sted programme, spanning from 2014 to the present. It includes photographs, project documentation, and materials from workshops, exhibitions, and excursions.

Images are served from the local archive server at Kgl. Akademi.
